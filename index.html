NovaTerm is a fast, beautiful SSH and SFTP client for Windows. GPU-accelerated terminals, tabs and split panes, port forwarding, and a full remote file browser — without leaving the keyboard.

Privacy policy Get support

SSH, done properly

Password, key, agent, Pageant, and jump hosts, with host-key verification and fingerprint prompts.

SFTP built in

Recursive upload and download, drag-and-drop, and a transfer queue with pause, resume, and cancel.

Local terminals

PowerShell sessions in the same tabbed window as your remote hosts, alongside port forwarding.

No telemetry

No analytics, no account, no tracking. Credentials are encrypted with Windows DPAPI.
