Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

In short

  • No telemetry, analytics, or advertising.
  • No account required.
  • Your data stays on your device, except for connections you choose to make.

NovaTerm is an SSH and SFTP desktop client published by Debashis Chakraborty. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Data stored on your device

NovaTerm stores the following locally:

  • Connection profiles (host, port, username, display preferences)
  • Credentials you choose to save, encrypted with the operating system's credential protection (Electron safeStorage: Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain, Linux keyring)
  • App settings, themes, and window layout
  • Optional session transcripts, written as plain text in the app's user-data folder when you enable logging

This data is not transmitted to the publisher. You can delete it by removing the app's user-data folder or uninstalling the app.

Network connections

NovaTerm makes network connections only when you initiate them:

  • SSH/SFTP sessions connect directly to the servers you enter.
  • Port forwarding tunnels traffic through those sessions.
  • Update checks (non-Store builds only) contact the update feed configured by the distributor. Microsoft Store builds do not self-update; the Store manages updates.

NovaTerm does not route your traffic through publisher-operated servers.

What the app does not collect

  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reporting
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contact lists or files, unless you explicitly transfer them over SFTP

Data deletion

Uninstalling NovaTerm removes the app. To delete saved profiles, credentials, settings, and transcripts, also remove the app's user-data folder (on Windows, under %APPDATA%\novaterm).

Children's privacy

NovaTerm does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
